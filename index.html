Iowa City's Sports Card Shop for Collectors of Every Level

Riverside Card Exchange is a small, collector-run shop in downtown Iowa City. We buy and sell football, basketball, baseball, and hockey cards, help you send your best cards out for grading, and check what your collection is worth for free. Whether you just found a shoebox of cards in your parents' basement or you've been chasing rookie cards for twenty years, you'll find someone here who wants to talk cards with you.

Get a free card value check

What we do

Buy and sell sports cards

We stock thousands of singles, sealed hobby boxes, and supplies. Browse our sports cards for sale, from $1 commons to graded rookie cards.

Card grading help

Not sure if a card is worth grading? We inspect corners, edges, centering, and surface before you pay a grading fee. Read our guide to getting sports cards graded.

Free value checks

Bring in up to 50 cards and we'll tell you which ones are worth more than a few dollars. Learn how to find out what your sports cards are worth.

Why collectors choose us

  • Honest offers. When we buy, we show you the recent sold prices we used to set our offer.
  • No pressure. A value check never obligates you to sell.
  • Beginner friendly. We explain terms like "rookie card," "parallel," and "serial numbered" without making you feel behind.
  • Local. Every trade night and box break happens in our shop, not on a livestream.

Common questions

Do you buy whole collections?

Yes. For collections larger than a few hundred cards, call ahead so we can set aside time to sort through them with you.

Do I need an appointment for a value check?

No. Walk in any time during store hours. Saturdays are busiest, so weekday afternoons are the quickest.

What sports do you carry?

Football, basketball, baseball, and hockey, with a small selection of soccer and racing cards.
